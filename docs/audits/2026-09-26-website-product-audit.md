# UniBlueprint Website — Product (Logged-In) Audit (2026-09-26)

Scope: the **website's logged-in / product surface only** (`src/`) — Foundation Blueprint builder pages, My Documents, Career Profile, Subscription Management/Success, GDPR self-service on the Privacy page, auth flows (sign-up/in, password reset, verify email), and the four staff surfaces (Founder, Operations, Finance dashboards; Handler Queue; Partner Portal). Public marketing pages and the mobile app (`app/`) are out of scope — both are covered by separate audits.

**This is a redo of an audit cut short on 2026-09-24.** No prior partial report existed to build on, so this is a full pass.

**Important scoping note:** this worktree's `main` branch was three weeks stale (last commit 2026-09-04) and contained none of the work this brief refers to — no CRO details, no GDPR self-service, none of today's migrations. All of that lives on `claude/website-build-ouh47r` (tip `93712be9`, dated 2026-09-26, 107 commits ahead of `main`). This audit was run against that branch's actual content (checked out locally as `audit-review-a1d9890`, pinned to `93712be9`), and the live Supabase project (`ozpeofkvnpikhopkgcxc`) was queried directly for the ground truth on tables, RPCs and Edge Functions, rather than trusting local migration files alone.

Method: every logged-in page/component under `src/` was read. Every table, RPC and Edge Function referenced from those pages was checked against the live Supabase project with read-only queries.

## 0. The headline: the DB is live, but the Edge Functions are not — every builder and every payment fails at the last step

The database side of the "entire Foundation Blueprint backend" is genuinely live: all 9 builder tables (`cv_documents`, `cover_letters`, `application_forms`, `interview_prep_packs`, `job_search_sessions`, `linkedin_documents`, `personal_statements`, `portfolio_plans`, `evidence_bank_stories`) exist, as does every handler-pipeline RPC (`submit_document_for_review`, `claim_submission`, `start_review`, `deliver_submission`, `flag_submission`, `mark_submission_incomplete`, `submit_handler_decision`, `fetch_operations_queue`, `list_active_handlers`, `reassign_submission`, `cancel_submission`, `contact_student`, `record_claim_attempt`), every Founder/Ops/Finance dashboard RPC, and the GDPR RPC (`export_my_gdpr_data`). This part of the app fix genuinely landed and the website's dashboards and Handler Queue are correctly wired to it (verified live, confirmed working).

**What is not live is the Edge Functions layer.** `mcp__Supabase__list_edge_functions` on project `ozpeofkvnpikhopkgcxc` returns exactly two deployed functions: `create-checkout-session` and `delete-account`. The other 14 defined under `supabase/functions/` are not deployed:

`generate-cv`, `generate-cover-letter`, `generate-application-answers`, `generate-interview-prep`, `generate-personal-statement`, `generate-portfolio-plan`, `generate-linkedin`, `generate-job-search-support`, `review-cv`, `review-cover-letter`, `stripe-webhook`, `create-portal-session`, `notify-team-submission`, `send-form-confirmation`, `sync-mailchimp-subscriber`.

Every one of these is called from a website page (see P0-1 through P0-3 below). This is why every Foundation builder, the free CV/cover-letter review tools, "Manage billing", and Pro checkout all fail on the website today, despite the DB being ready underneath them.

## Ordered fix list

### P0 — broken core product, real money, or fabricated data shown as real

**P0-1 — Every Foundation Blueprint builder fails at "Generate".**
`src/pages/foundation/CvBuilderPage.jsx:281`, `CoverLetterBuilderPage.jsx:182`, `ApplicationFormPage.jsx:340`, `InterviewPrepPage.jsx:177`, `PersonalStatementPage.jsx:182`, `PortfolioBuildingPage.jsx:162`, `LinkedInOptimisationPage.jsx:187`, `JobSearchSupportPage.jsx:190`, `CareerProfilePage.jsx:259,300,331`, plus the free-standing review tools `CvReviewPage.jsx:34` and `CoverLetterReviewPage.jsx:33` — every one calls `invokeFunction('generate-*'|'review-*', …)`, and none of those 10 functions are deployed (section 0). A student can complete a 10–15 question flow, including the newer draft-autosave/resume logic (`CvBuilderPage.jsx:103-140`), and hits a hard failure exactly at the point of value. The error is surfaced cleanly (`src/lib/invokeFunction.js` correctly unwraps the function's real error body, so this is not a blank crash), and the in-progress row is left recoverable as a `draft` in My Documents — but the product does not work.
*Fix:* deploy the 10 `generate-*`/`review-*` functions with their secrets (AI provider key, `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`), then verify each once end-to-end. Until then, consider a maintenance banner on `/foundation/*` rather than letting users hit this after 10+ questions.

**P0-2 — Paying customers see "No active subscription found" on the page meant to confirm their payment, and never become Pro.**
`src/pages/SubscriptionSuccessPage.jsx:1-2` carries its own TODO: *"Stripe webhook updates user Pro status in Supabase. Remove manual check once webhook is live."* Since `stripe-webhook` is not deployed (section 0), no `subscriptions` row is ever created after a real Stripe Checkout completes, so the manual check at `SubscriptionSuccessPage.jsx:17-31` always resolves to `status === 'none'`, rendering the red "No active subscription found" panel (`:97-136`) to someone who just paid via `create-checkout-session` (which *is* deployed and will genuinely redirect to Stripe and take the card). The same gap means `SubscriptionManagementPage.jsx` and the Navbar's Pro badge (via `AuthContext.jsx:98-100`'s `isPro`) never flip for a real paying customer either.
*Fix:* deploy `stripe-webhook` with the correct signing secret and endpoint URL configured in the Stripe dashboard. This is the single highest-priority function to deploy — it is real money changing hands with no product delivered.

**P0-3 — Foundation Blueprint has no payment step at all; every service is free to submit.**
`src/components/foundation/TierPicker.jsx:1-7`'s own comment says it: *"chosen at the point of submission — not inferred from payment, since nothing currently charges for Premium."* No Foundation page ever references `isPro` (confirmed absent from every file in `src/pages/foundation/`), and there is no checkout call anywhere in the builder flow before `submitForReview()`. Meanwhile `PricingPage.jsx:17-24` lists `'Foundation Blueprint (all services)'` and `'Elevation Blueprint (all services)'` as `PRO_FEATURES`, and the same page's Section 3 "Every service. Every price." (`:457-535`) shows a per-service price (€8–€40) for each of the 9 Foundation/Elevation services with **no checkout button anywhere for an individual service** — it's purely informational. Every one of these paid products is fully free and unlimited on the live website today.
*Fix:* this needs a product decision before a fix (see D1). Either gate the builder behind `isPro`, or add a real per-service checkout (extend `create-checkout-session` to accept a one-off service line item) before `submit_document_for_review` is called.

**P0-4 — Partner Portal (`/portal/partner`) shows 100% fabricated data to every real business partner who logs in.**
`src/pages/portal/PartnerPortalPage.jsx:20` — `// DEMO DATA, replace with a live Supabase query scoped to the logged-in partner's own partner_id once the analytics schema is populated` — and the whole 650-line file backs that up: there is not one `supabase.from(...)` or `supabase.rpc(...)` call anywhere in it (confirmed by grep). The fabricated `DEMO` object (`:20-190`) invents a specific fictional business ("MPFitness", Fitness category, `milanpir_fitness` Instagram handle) with fake weekly views/claims/CTR deltas, and every real partner or founder who is routed here via `RequireRole allow={['business','operations','founder','admin']}` (`App.jsx:282-289`) sees this same fictional company's numbers presented as their own live analytics. This is materially worse than showing zeros: it is fabricated commercial performance data shown as real to a paying advertiser, and the DB already has the real events to back a real version of this page — `partner_deal_viewed`/`partner_deal_claimed` are logged and correctly read by `FounderDashboardPage.jsx:24,28-29` in the exact same codebase.
*Fix:* rebuild this page against `partners`, `deals`, and the `partner_deal_viewed`/`partner_deal_claimed` event stream, scoped to the logged-in partner's own `partner_id` (via `partner_users`). Until that ships, gate the route or show an honest "Analytics coming soon" state instead of someone else's fake numbers.

**P0-5 — Checkout charges more than the price shown next to the button.**
`src/pages/PricingPage.jsx:168,241,346-368,412-423` renders a "Free Trial" toggle that shows €3.50/mo and €24.99/yr (struck through against €6.99/€49.99) directly above the "Get Pro"/"Get Premium" buttons. Those buttons (`CheckoutButton.jsx:34-36`) only ever pass `{ tier }`, with no trial flag, into `create-checkout-session`, whose `PRICING` map (`supabase/functions/create-checkout-session/index.ts:31-34`) has exactly two entries, both at the standard/full price — and the function's own comment admits it (`:26-30`): *"this always charges the standard `amount`, not the 50%-off free trial price shown on the Pricing page... otherwise the site advertises €3.50/€24.99 but Stripe charges full price at checkout."* A customer who toggles to "Free Trial", sees €3.50, and clicks Get Pro is charged €6.99 — double the advertised price, with no confirmation step in between. This is a real billing-accuracy problem for a now-formally-registered company (UniBlueprint Limited, CRO 826545), not just a copy issue.
*Fix:* either wire `trialAmount` through to a real trial-priced Stripe line item (pass a trial flag from `CheckoutButton`, add trial entries to the function's `PRICING` map, keyed correctly to an actual trial end date), or remove the Free Trial toggle from Pricing until it can honestly charge what it shows.

**P0-6 — Self-service account deletion never cancels the Stripe subscription; a deleted user keeps being billed with no way to stop it.**
`supabase/functions/delete-account/index.ts` deletes the Supabase auth user (`:79`, cascades every DB row including the local `subscriptions` row) but never calls the Stripe API to cancel the underlying subscription. This function is the same one the new website GDPR self-service now calls (`src/pages/legal/PrivacyPage.jsx:177`, added by the "self-service GDPR export/delete" work). A Pro subscriber who uses the new "Delete my account" button on `/privacy` loses their account, their Handler-visible history, and their Stripe portal access in the same click — but Stripe keeps charging their card every billing cycle, with no account left to log into and cancel from. This compounds P0-2/P0-5: real money keeps moving with the customer relationship already severed.
*Fix:* in `delete-account`, look up the user's `subscriptions.stripe_subscription_id` (or Stripe customer by email) and call `stripe.subscriptions.cancel(...)` before or alongside `admin.auth.admin.deleteUser(user.id)`. Log the cancellation result to `gdpr_deletion_log`.

### P1 — broken or actively misleading, fix in the same pass

**P1-1 — Sign-in and post-payment copy tell users the Foundation/Elevation builders "are in the app", when the website has its own (more complete) builder.**
`src/pages/auth/SignInPage.jsx:60` — *"Your Foundation and Elevation Blueprint tools are in the UniBlueprint app, sign in there with the same email and password."* `src/pages/SubscriptionSuccessPage.jsx:62-63,89` repeats the same claim ("Open the app") right after a Pro purchase made on the website. Both are directly contradicted by the same codebase: `/foundation/cv-builder`, `/cover-letter`, `/application-form-assistance`, `/interview-preparation`, `/personal-statement`, `/portfolio-building`, `/linkedin-optimisation`, `/job-search-support`, `/career-profile` and `/my-documents` are all real, working (once P0-1 is fixed) website routes with draft autosave and a pipeline status tracker (`CvBuilderPage.jsx`, `PipelineStatusTimeline` import) — arguably the more advanced implementation of the two platforms. A website Pro subscriber who reads either page will bounce to the app store instead of using the tools already in their browser.
*Fix:* rewrite both copy blocks to point at `/foundation/my-documents` and `/foundation/career-profile` on the website itself; drop the app hand-off entirely or make it opt-in ("also available in our app").

**P1-2 — No navigation entry point to My Documents, Career Profile, or any of the 4 staff dashboards.**
`src/components/layout/Navbar.jsx:261-286` (desktop) and `:767-793` (mobile) — the signed-in account menu has exactly two entries: "My Account" (→ `/subscription-management`) and "Sign Out". There is no link to `/foundation/my-documents` or `/foundation/career-profile` anywhere outside `FoundationBlueprintPage.jsx:700,710` (confirmed by repo-wide grep — those are the only two references to either route). There is likewise no link anywhere in the site to `/admin/founder`, `/admin/operations`, `/admin/finance`, `/handler/queue`, or `/portal/partner` — staff must know or bookmark the exact URL.
*Fix:* add "My Documents" to the account dropdown for every signed-in user, and a role-conditional "Dashboard" link (using `useUserRole`/`portalRole` from `AuthContext.jsx:102`) for handler/operations/founder/business roles.

**P1-3 — No admin surface on the website reviews `gdpr_requests`.**
Confirmed by grep: no file under `src/pages/admin/`, `src/pages/handler/`, or `src/pages/portal/` references `gdpr_requests`. The new self-service export/delete flow (`PrivacyPage.jsx`) correctly bypasses the queue for logged-in users, but the manual form on the same page (`PrivacyPage.jsx:80-123`, used by logged-out visitors and for correction/restriction requests from anyone) writes into `gdpr_requests` with nowhere on the website for Operations to see, triage, or mark it complete — the only mechanism is the `notify_ops_on_gdpr_request` trigger's email, and the 30-day statutory clock (`set_gdpr_due_at`) starts ticking whether or not anyone reads that email.
*Fix:* add a GDPR requests panel to `OperationsDashboardPage.jsx` (list, status, mark-complete), reusing the same `gdpr_requests`/`gdpr_deletion_log` tables the mobile app's equivalent screen already uses.

**P1-4 — Auth state never handles a failed/rejected fetch; a transient error leaves the UI stuck.**
`src/context/AuthContext.jsx:61-70` — `supabase.auth.getSession().then(...)` has no `.catch()`, so a rejected promise (network blip, storage error) leaves `loading` at `true` forever, which every `ProtectedRoute`/`RequireRole` interprets as "still loading" and shows a permanent spinner. `loadRolesAndSubscription` (`:18-32`) likewise never checks `{ error }` on either query — a transient RLS/network failure on the `subscriptions` fetch silently sets `subscription` to `null`, dropping a real Pro user to the free-tier UI until their next full page load with no user-visible error at all.
*Fix:* add `.catch(() => setLoading(false))` to the `getSession` chain, and check `roleRows`/`subRow` errors in `loadRolesAndSubscription`, keeping the previous state (or showing an inline error) rather than silently zeroing it.

**P1-5 — Marketing-consent sign-up checkbox silently does nothing.**
`src/pages/auth/SignUpPage.jsx:52` calls `syncMailchimp(...)` when the consent checkbox is ticked; `syncMailchimp` (`src/components/ui/Form.jsx:29-33`) invokes `sync-mailchimp-subscriber`, which is not deployed (section 0) and fails silently by design (`.catch(err => console.warn(...))`, non-blocking so it never breaks sign-up). The effect: every user who ticks "Send me marketing emails" believes they've opted in, and Privacy Policy `PrivacyPage.jsx:338,361` promises this is how marketing consent is honoured, but no subscriber is ever actually created in Mailchimp.
*Fix:* deploy `sync-mailchimp-subscriber` (bundled with the other function deployments in P0-1) and confirm a real Mailchimp API key/list ID is set as a function secret.

### P2 — polish, consistency, and consumer-protection wording

**P2-1 — Orphaned `draft` rows accumulate for every user who hits P0-1 before the functions are deployed.**
By design (`CvBuilderPage.jsx:267-281` and the equivalent in every other builder), the document row is inserted/updated *before* `invokeFunction('generate-*', …)` is called, so a failed generation leaves a real `draft` row behind. This is a deliberate, sound design for recoverability (visible and deletable via `MyDocumentsPage.jsx:27-32,73-77`) — flagging only because until P0-1 ships, every real attempt today creates one of these, so a bulk cleanup of accumulated test/failed drafts may be worth doing once functions are deployed.

**P2-2 — Founder/Operations/Finance dashboards and Handler Queue: no functional issues found.**
For the record, since this is an area the app audit flagged issues in: every RPC referenced by `FounderDashboardPage.jsx`, `OperationsDashboardPage.jsx`, `FinanceDashboardPage.jsx`, and `HandlerQueuePage.jsx`/`src/lib/handlerQueue.js` was confirmed to exist live (`get_founder_topline`, `get_feature_engagement`, `get_weekly_signups`, `get_all_partner_stats`, `get_coach_enquiry_counts`, `get_campus_breakdown`, `get_retention_snapshot`, `get_ops_queue_snapshot`, `get_ops_queue_times`, `get_handler_roster`, `get_sunday_queue_status`, `get_campus_demand`, `get_service_demand`, `get_tier_mix`, `get_finance_revenue_snapshot`, `get_finance_commission_summary`, `get_finance_partner_payout_summary`, plus the full claim/review/deliver/escalation RPC set). Error handling is proper (`Promise.all` + explicit `.error` checks, `EMPTY_*` fallback states, no fabricated deltas — `FounderDashboardPage.jsx:9-13` explicitly documents deciding *against* a fake trend line for exactly this reason). This part of the rebuild is solid and should be the reference implementation for fixing Partner Portal (P0-4).

**P2-3 — GDPR self-service (export + delete) works end-to-end for a logged-in user, with one caveat.**
Verified: `export_my_gdpr_data` RPC exists live and is called correctly (`PrivacyPage.jsx:148`), producing a downloaded JSON file client-side with no server round-trip beyond the RPC. `delete-account` is deployed live and is called correctly (`:177`) with a confirm step (`:208-259`) before it fires. The only real gap is P0-6 (Stripe never cancelled) — everything else in this flow is genuinely wired to the live backend, which is the correct headline for this specific feature.

**P2-4 — Data consistency between website and mobile app: no divergence found, but both share the same root failure.**
Both platforms read the identical `subscriptions` table with the identical `isPro` formula (`AuthContext.jsx:98-100` on web matches the mobile app's equivalent). Since neither platform's `stripe-webhook` writes to that table, a paying user shows as free on *both* — consistently wrong, not divergently wrong. No cross-platform data mismatch was found beyond that shared cause; fixing P0-2 fixes it everywhere at once.

**P2-5 — "Every service. Every price." table on Pricing has no purchase path.**
Noted separately from P0-3 because it's also a pure UX dead end regardless of the payment-model decision: `PricingPage.jsx:507-535`'s per-service table (CV Optimisation €20/€10, Job Search Support €15/€8, etc.) has no button, link, or CTA of any kind attached to any row — it's read-only. Even a logged-out visitor comparing prices has nowhere to click through from this table to `/foundation-blueprint`.
*Fix:* link each row (or the section) to its matching `/foundation/*` builder, at minimum, independent of whether a real checkout is added.

## Decisions needed

- **D1 — What actually gates Foundation/Elevation access, and how is an individual service paid for?** (P0-3) Options: (a) subscription-gate the builder behind `isPro` and drop per-service pricing from Pricing entirely; (b) keep per-service free-standing pricing and add a real one-off Stripe checkout per submission, independent of the Pro subscription; (c) some hybrid (Pro = discount + priority, service fee still applies). This decision blocks the fix for P0-3 and P2-5.
- **D2 — Free Trial pricing: fix the checkout to match, or remove the toggle?** (P0-5) If the free trial is meant to keep running, `create-checkout-session` needs real trial-priced line items tied to an actual trial end date (not indefinite 50% off). If the trial window has effectively passed, simplest fix is removing the toggle and the struck-through pricing from `PricingPage.jsx` entirely.
- **D3 — Partner Portal rebuild priority.** (P0-4) This is a founder/business-facing surface actively showing fabricated numbers to (potentially) real paying partners today. Given `partner_deal_viewed`/`partner_deal_claimed` events and the `partners`/`deals` tables already exist and are read correctly elsewhere (Founder Dashboard), this should be treated as a near-term rebuild, not backlog — confirm before the next partner demo or renewal conversation happens on this page.
- **D4 — Deployment order for the 14 missing Edge Functions.** (P0-1, P0-2, P0-5 partially, P1-5) Recommend: `stripe-webhook` first (money), then the 8 `generate-*` functions (core product), then `review-cv`/`review-cover-letter` and `create-portal-session`, then `notify-team-submission`/`send-form-confirmation`/`sync-mailchimp-subscriber` (notifications, non-blocking today). Confirm all required secrets (Stripe live keys + webhook signing secret, AI provider key, Resend key, Mailchimp API key/list ID) are set before each deploy, not after.
