## Rules for using partner marks in the app and marketing
- Use the partner's own logo files and colours exactly as published, never redraw them.
- Say only what the partner's own site says about itself. No rates, promises or claims on their behalf.
- Kobo is in closed beta (test money only, waitlist only) as of 19 November 2026. The card says "Join the Kobo waitlist" until Kobo says otherwise.
- If a partner asks to be removed, remove the mark and the link in the next release and from the website.
