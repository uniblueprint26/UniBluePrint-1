# Creators Top 10s and UBP Awards nominees: sources

All pages below were opened on 23 November 2026 (WebFetch or direct page fetch). "Official profile" means the public TikTok profile page of the creator, opened the same day, which gives the follower count shown. Search snippets were not used as facts. Gender (she/her, he/him) is stated by each person's opened source as listed; brothers and lads are groups placed in the male list.

Method: the founder's three keepers were checked first. The wider field came from the influencer.ie TikTok ranking (updated July 2026, 33 creators with county and dated reach), the Favikon Ireland TikTok ranking (7 May 2026), the RTÉ and Irish Examiner TikTok Awards UK and Ireland 2025 shortlist coverage, and the TikTok Awards UK and Ireland 2025 winners page.

## Ranking and shortlist sources used for all names
- influencer.ie TikTok ranking, https://influencer.ie/platform/tiktok/ . Confirmed: "last updated July 2026", ranked by reported reach, each creator with county and an "as of" date. Individual profile pages under https://influencer.ie/influencers/NAME/ also opened (county, niche, reach and date, bio line). influencer.ie does not state gender.
- Favikon, Top 20 TikTokers in Ireland, https://www.favikon.com/blog/top-tiktokers-ireland . Confirmed: published 7 May 2026, Ciaran McDonnell ranked 9 of 20, "comedic content creator", "his humorous TikTok videos", "millions of likes".
- RTÉ, 12 Irish content creators nominated for TikTok Awards 2025 (16 Oct 2025), https://www.rte.ie/lifestyle/living/2025/1016/1538876-12-irish-content-creators-nominated-for-tiktok-awards-2025 . Confirmed the twelve Irish nominees.
- RTÉ, Irish creators turn red carpet green at London's TikTok Awards (15 Nov 2025), https://www.rte.ie/lifestyle/living/2025/1115/1544075-irish-creators-turn-red-carpet-green-at-londons-tiktok-awards/ . Confirmed Matthew and Michael Gardiner ("they"), Miriam Mullins ("her"), Brad Bennett and Aghogho Okpara.
- TikTok newsroom, winners of the TikTok Awards UK and Ireland 2025 (13 Nov 2025). Confirmed the Gardiner Brothers were Highly Commended for Video of the Year. No Irish creator won a category that year, so none of the picks is described as a winner.
