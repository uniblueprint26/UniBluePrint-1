# UniBlueprint (UBP): product vocabulary

Read this before writing any user-facing copy or designing any screen.

## Course Connect is not about academic courses
"Course" here means a course of life, a journey, and it is wordplay: everyone on Course Connect is on the same course, working toward getting better and a common goal. It is a nationwide, cross-Ireland network and the default place a post goes. People on it may be students, apprentices or workers, in different places and doing different things. Never describe it as "people on your course" or tie it to a subject, degree or module.

## The audiences for a post
- **Course Connect**: public across Ireland. Always the default and always listed first.
- **Campus Connect**: your own community in the same situation. For college students it is their campus. Apprentices get their own version (for example a trade-based group), and workers get theirs (by industry). Wording must not assume everyone attends college.
- **Friends only**: mutual follows.

## Pillars, names and icons
Foundation = file/envelope, Elevation = arrow, Lifestyle = heart, Campus Connect = building, Course Connect = Irish flag. Post types are Mood Board, Post, Clip, Live. Never use the word "Thread" in the UI.

## Copy and look
Plain, short sentences. No em-dashes. Clean, never bunched up. Gold is only for money. Light and dark must both work. Seeded content is labelled "From UBP" or "Posted by UBP".

## Pricing (real)
Pro is 6.99 euro a month or 49.99 euro a year. All services are half price during the free trial, and the trial is free.

## Ad Board
Order: Weekly Blueprint magazine at the top (the headline the founder approved), then this week's blogs, then Post a listing, then the Marketplace as a set of baskets (Deals, Jobs, Tutors and so on). Ride shares and Lost and found belong in Campus Connect, not the Ad Board. Never sell Campus Connect features on the Ad Board.
