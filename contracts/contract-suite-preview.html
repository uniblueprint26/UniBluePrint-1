Two things to resolve before anything is sent

1.The coach revenue share is already public

1.1The website Join page states that Uni Coaches "Keep 85% of every booking". That is a public commitment made to every coach who has read it.
1.2Document 03 is drafted at 85% coach / 15% UniBlueprint to match. Clause 7 then gives you a mechanism to confirm or change the rate by 25 September 2026, with the coach free to walk away if the change is less favourable.
1.3If you want a different split, change the website first. Issuing a contract at a worse rate than your published promise is the kind of inconsistency that loses coaches and invites a consumer-protection complaint.

2.You are not incorporated yet

2.1Every template carries [COMPANY NUMBER] and [REGISTERED OFFICE] placeholders. Until UniBlueprint Limited exists, there is no company to contract with.
2.2Document 01 clause 2 handles this: you sign personally and for the company to be formed, and the company ratifies within thirty days of incorporation under section 45 of the Companies Act 2014.
2.3Practical order: incorporate, then fill the company number and registered office into every template, then issue. If you must issue before incorporation, use the same pre-incorporation wording from document 01 clause 2 in each contract, and ratify them all at the first board meeting.

Employment status: the single biggest legal risk

Every role in this suite is drafted as independent contracting. Irish law decides status on substance, not on the label in the contract. Since Revenue Commissioners v Karshan (Midlands) Ltd t/a Domino's Pizza [2023] IESC 24, the test asks five questions: is there a wage-work bargain, is there mutuality of obligation, does the employer control the work, do the facts as a whole point to self-employment, and does any legislation change the answer.

If the WRC, Revenue, or a court decides a role is really employment, the consequences fall on UniBlueprint: unpaid employer PRSI with interest, holiday pay under the Organisation of Working Time Act 1997, minimum wage under the National Minimum Wage Act 2000, and potential unfair dismissal exposure.

Garda vetting: start this now

aUniBlueprint serves people under 18 through Course Compass, CAO personal statements, college interview prep, and course selection guidance.
bUnder the National Vetting Bureau (Children and Vulnerable Persons) Acts 2012 to 2016 it is a criminal offence to permit a person to do relevant work with children without a vetting disclosure.
cUniBlueprint must first register as a relevant organisation with the National Vetting Bureau before it can seek vetting for anyone. That registration takes time. Begin it now.
dUntil vetting is in place, either restrict those services to over-18s and enforce it in the product, or do not run them.
eNote the tension in your current documents: the Terms of Service say users must be 18 or over, but Course Compass and CAO services are aimed at 5th and 6th year students. Resolve that before launch. Either the age gate is real and enforced, or vetting is required.

Parties

(1)UniBlueprint Limited, a company to be incorporated / incorporated in Ireland (registered number [COMPANY NUMBER]) whose registered office is at [REGISTERED OFFICE] (the "Company").
(2)[FOUNDER FULL NAME] of [ADDRESS], date of birth [DOB] (the "Founder").

Sign this one first, and sign it even if nothing else gets signed

Clause 6 assigns to the Company everything already built: the website, the app, the UniBlueprint name and brand, designs, copy, and data. Until that assignment exists in writing, those assets belong to the Founder personally, not the Company. Every investor, grant body, and acquirer will ask for this document. If the Company is not yet incorporated, clause 2 handles it.

Background

A. The Founder conceived UniBlueprint and has, before the date of this Agreement, personally created or commissioned the UniBlueprint brand, website, mobile application, designs, written content, and business materials (together, the "Pre-Existing Assets").

B. The Company is being formed, or has been formed, to carry on the UniBlueprint business.

C. The parties wish to record the Founder's role, to transfer the Pre-Existing Assets to the Company, and to set out how the Founder's shares vest.

1.Definitions

1.1In this Agreement:
(a)"Board" means the board of directors of the Company.
(b)"Cliff Date" means the first anniversary of the Start Date.
(c)"Founder Shares" means the shares in the Company issued or to be issued to the Founder, as recorded in Schedule 2.
(d)"Good Leaver" means the Founder ceasing to provide services by reason of death, permanent incapacity, or termination by the Company other than for Cause.
(e)"Bad Leaver" means the Founder ceasing to provide services by resignation before the fourth anniversary of the Start Date, or termination by the Company for Cause.
(f)"Cause" means material breach of this Agreement not remedied within fourteen (14) days, dishonesty or fraud, conviction of an indictable offence, or conduct seriously damaging to the Company.
(g)"Start Date" means [START DATE].

2.Pre-incorporation

2.1Where this Agreement is entered into before the Company is incorporated, the Founder enters into it both personally and for and on behalf of the Company to be formed.
2.2The Company will, within thirty (30) days of incorporation, ratify this Agreement by board resolution in accordance with section 45 of the Companies Act 2014, and this Agreement will then take effect between the Company and the Founder as if the Company had been a party from the date first written above.
2.3Until ratification the Founder is not personally liable to any third party under this Agreement beyond what the law requires.
2.4If the Company is not incorporated within twelve (12) months of the date of this Agreement, either party may terminate it by written notice, and the assignment in clause 6 does not take effect.

3.Role

3.1The Founder is appointed as [Founder and Chief Executive / Founder and Managing Director] of the Company with effect from the Start Date.
3.2The Founder's responsibilities are set out in Schedule 1 and include setting the Company's strategy and direction, leading product and brand, building and leading the team, managing partner and institutional relationships, and representing the Company externally.
3.3The Founder will devote such time to the Company as is reasonably necessary, being not less than [NUMBER] hours per week, and will use reasonable skill, care, and diligence.
3.4The Founder may continue in full-time education and may hold other interests, provided they do not compete with the Company and do not materially interfere with the Founder's obligations. The Founder's existing interests are disclosed in Schedule 3.
3.5The Founder will comply with the Company's constitution, any shareholders' agreement, and all applicable law.

4.Director duties

4.1Where the Founder is appointed a director, the Founder will comply with the duties of a director under section 228 of the Companies Act 2014, including to act in good faith in what the Founder considers to be the interests of the Company, to act honestly and responsibly, to act in accordance with the constitution and exercise powers only for lawful purposes, not to benefit or use Company property for the Founder or anyone else unless approved, not to fetter their discretion, to avoid conflicts of interest, to exercise care skill and diligence, and to have regard to the interests of members and employees.

4.3The Founder will not, without Board approval, enter the Company into any transaction outside the ordinary course of business, any commitment exceeding €[THRESHOLD], any borrowing, any grant of security, or any transaction in which the Founder has a personal interest.
4.4Resignation or removal as a director does not of itself terminate this Agreement, and termination of this Agreement does not of itself remove the Founder as a director.

5.Remuneration

5.1The Founder's remuneration is set out in Schedule 2. Where Schedule 2 records that no remuneration is payable, the Founder acknowledges that they provide services without payment and that no debt accrues, unless the Board resolves otherwise.
5.2The Board may resolve to pay the Founder a salary or fee once the Company has sufficient resources. Any such payment must be approved by the Board and recorded.
5.3The Company will reimburse expenses properly and necessarily incurred on Company business, on production of receipts, in accordance with any expenses policy.
5.4Where any remuneration becomes payable, the Company will operate PAYE, PRSI, and USC as required, or the Founder will account for tax as a self-employed person, depending on the basis recorded in Schedule 2.

6.Assignment of the Pre-Existing Assets

6.1The Founder assigns to the Company, with full title guarantee and free of all encumbrances, all right, title, and interest in the Pre-Existing Assets and all intellectual property in them, throughout the world and for the full term of those rights, including all rights of action for past infringement.

(a)the name "UniBlueprint", the "UBP" mark, all logos, wordmarks, and brand assets, and all goodwill in them;
(b)the source code, designs, and content of the UniBlueprint website and mobile application;
(c)all written copy, policies, service descriptions, and marketing materials;
(d)all designs, illustrations, photographs, and video created for or used by UniBlueprint;
(e)all domain names, social media accounts and handles, and app store listings;
(f)all databases, member records, partner records, and business data; and
(g)all business plans, financial models, and know-how relating to the business.
6.3The Founder waives all moral rights in the Pre-Existing Assets so far as the law allows.
6.4The Founder will, at the Company's cost, sign any further document and do anything else reasonably required to perfect the assignment, including any assignment required by a domain registrar, app store, or intellectual property registry. This obligation continues after termination.
6.5The Founder warrants that they own the Pre-Existing Assets, that they are free to assign them, that no third party has any claim to them, and that they do not infringe any third party right. Where any element was created by a third party, the Founder has obtained a written assignment or will procure one.
6.6All intellectual property the Founder creates on or after the Start Date in connection with the Company's business belongs to the Company, and clause 6.1 applies to it as if it were a Pre-Existing Asset.

Third-party contributions need their own assignment

If anyone else wrote code, drew a logo, took photographs, or wrote copy for UniBlueprint, clause 6.5 is not satisfied by this document alone. Each of those people must sign the standalone IP Assignment Deed (document 12) covering what they made. Do this before any investor due diligence.

7.Founder Shares and vesting

7.1The Founder holds or will be issued the Founder Shares set out in Schedule 2.
7.2The Founder Shares vest over four (4) years from the Start Date as follows: twenty-five per cent (25%) vests on the Cliff Date, and the remainder vests in equal monthly instalments over the following thirty-six (36) months.
7.3If the Founder ceases to provide services before the Cliff Date, no Founder Shares vest and all Founder Shares are subject to transfer under clause 7.5.
7.4If the Founder ceases to provide services after the Cliff Date, the Founder retains the vested portion and the unvested portion is subject to transfer under clause 7.5.
7.5On a leaver event the Company or its nominee may require the transfer of the relevant Founder Shares:
(a)where the Founder is a Good Leaver, at fair market value as determined by the Company's auditors acting as experts;
(b)where the Founder is a Bad Leaver, at the lower of the subscription price paid and fair market value.
7.6On a sale of the Company or a listing, all unvested Founder Shares vest in full immediately before completion.
7.7This clause is subject to any shareholders' agreement, which prevails in the event of conflict.

Vesting protects the Founder too

Vesting is not only an investor protection. It is what stops an early co-founder who leaves after two months from keeping a large block of the company. Fill Schedule 2 with the actual share numbers before signing, and take tax advice on the share issue before it happens, not after.

8.Confidentiality

8.1The Founder will keep confidential all information relating to the Company's business, and will not use it other than for the Company's benefit, during this Agreement and after it ends.
8.2This does not prevent a protected disclosure under the Protected Disclosures Act 2014 or any disclosure required by law.

9.Restrictions

9.1While providing services and for twelve (12) months afterwards, the Founder will not:
(a)carry on or be engaged in any business in Ireland that competes directly with the Company's business as carried on at the date of termination;
(b)solicit any partner, coach, handler, or team member of the Company with whom the Founder dealt in the twelve (12) months before termination; or
(c)solicit any member or customer of the Company for a competing service.
9.2These restrictions are limited to what is reasonably necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests. If any is held excessive, it applies with the minimum modification necessary to make it enforceable.
9.3Nothing in clause 9.1 prevents the Founder from holding not more than three per cent (3%) of a listed company, or from continuing any interest disclosed in Schedule 3.

10.Termination

10.1This Agreement continues until terminated.
10.2Either party may terminate on three (3) months' written notice.
10.3The Company may terminate immediately for Cause.
10.4On termination the Founder will deliver up all Company property, data, and credentials, and will resign as a director if the Board so requests.
10.5Clauses 6, 7, 8, 9, and 11 survive termination.

11.General

11.1This Agreement, with its Schedules, is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter, subject to any shareholders' agreement.
11.2Any variation must be in writing and signed.
11.3The Founder may not assign this Agreement.
11.4If any provision is held unenforceable it is severed and the remainder continues.
11.5This Agreement is governed by the laws of Ireland and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Ireland.

4.2Do not promise confidentiality to a member who discloses risk of harm. Explain that the concern must be passed on.
4.3Support the member calmly and without judgement. Do not minimise what they have said and do not tell them they are overreacting.

5.Professional boundaries

5.1Everyone acting for UniBlueprint will:
(a)communicate with members only through UniBlueprint systems, and not through personal phone numbers, personal social accounts, or private messaging;
(b)keep communication relevant to the service being provided;
(c)never arrange to meet a member privately or one-to-one in a non-public setting, and never at a private residence;
(d)never pursue or accept a romantic or sexual relationship with a member met through UniBlueprint;
(e)never give or accept a gift, loan, or money to or from a member;
(f)never take, request, or share a photograph or recording of a member without documented consent, and never of a person under 18 without the consent of a parent or guardian;
(g)never share personal contact details, home address, or social media with a member; and
(h)never be alone with a person under 18, whether in person or in a private online session.
5.2In-person sessions must take place in an appropriate professional or public setting. Where a Coach delivers in-person training, the venue must be a recognised commercial or sporting facility.
5.3If a member attempts to move the relationship outside these boundaries, decline politely and report it.

6.Respect and equality

6.1Everyone will treat members and colleagues with dignity and respect, and will not discriminate, harass, or victimise on any ground protected by the Employment Equality Acts 1998 to 2015 or the Equal Status Acts 2000 to 2018, being gender, civil status, family status, sexual orientation, religion, age, disability, race, and membership of the Traveller community.
6.2Bullying, harassment, sexual harassment, and abuse of any kind are prohibited and will result in immediate termination.
6.3Use inclusive language. Refer to the community as "young people". Do not assume anyone's pathway, background, gender, or circumstances.
6.4Anyone who experiences or witnesses harassment should report it to the Founder or the Designated Liaison Person. Reports are taken seriously and handled confidentially so far as possible.

7.Scope of competence

7.1No one will give advice outside their competence and qualifications.
7.2In particular, no one will provide medical, psychological, psychiatric, therapeutic, financial, investment, immigration, or legal advice unless they are qualified, registered, and insured to do so.
7.3Where a member needs support outside the scope of the service, refer them to an appropriate professional or to the resources in the app.
7.4Never present a personal opinion as professional advice.

8.Online conduct and community spaces

8.1Community boards, chats, and direct messages are moderated. Anyone with moderation access will act promptly on reported content.
8.2Prohibited content includes anything that is abusive, harassing, discriminatory, sexual, violent, promotes self-harm, promotes an age-restricted product, or discloses another person's personal information.
8.3Report rather than engage. Do not argue with a user in a public space.
8.4Preserve evidence before removing content where the matter may need to be reported.

9.Confidentiality and member information

9.1Member information is confidential. Access only what the task requires, do not discuss a member's circumstances with anyone outside the authorised channel, and do not copy information out of the platform.
9.2Confidentiality does not apply where there is a risk of harm or a child protection concern. Safety comes first.
9.3The Data Processing Terms set out the detailed obligations and apply in full.

10.Disclosure obligations

10.1Everyone must disclose immediately to UniBlueprint:
(a)any criminal charge or conviction;
(b)any Garda investigation involving them;
(c)any professional or academic disciplinary finding;
(d)any child protection concern raised about them in any setting; and
(e)any change affecting the validity of their vetting.
10.2Failure to disclose is itself a serious breach.

11.Reporting and response

11.1Concerns are reported to the Designated Liaison Person named in the Annex, or where the concern involves that person, directly to the Founder.
11.2UniBlueprint will acknowledge a report promptly, take immediate protective steps where needed including suspending access, and refer to Tusla or An Garda Síochána where required.
11.3A person who is the subject of a safeguarding concern may be suspended while it is assessed. Suspension is a neutral protective step and is not a finding.
11.4Records of concerns and responses are kept securely and separately, and retained in line with the retention schedule.

12.Breach

12.1Breach of this Policy is a material breach of the person's agreement with UniBlueprint and may result in immediate termination.
12.2Serious breaches will be reported to An Garda Síochána, Tusla, or a professional regulator as appropriate.
